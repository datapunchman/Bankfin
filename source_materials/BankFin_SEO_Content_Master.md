# 19. FAQ PAGE

## SEO title

**Loan & Financing FAQs \| BankFin**

## Meta description

**Get answers to common questions about personal loans, home loans,
business loans, MSME finance, overdrafts, project finance and loans
against property.**

## H1

**Frequently Asked Questions**

### What types of loans and financing does BankFin help with?

BankFin currently presents personal loans, home loans, unsecured
business loans, MSME loans, overdraft facilities, project finance, and
loans against property.

### Can BankFin guarantee loan approval?

No. Final approval is determined by the respective lender based on its
eligibility, credit, income, documentation, verification, and
risk-assessment criteria.

### What documents are required?

Documentation varies by product, applicant profile, lender, and purpose.
Common requirements may include identity/address proof, income or
business documents, bank statements, tax records, and property documents
where applicable.

### How much loan can I get?

The eligible amount depends on factors such as income, repayment
capacity, credit profile, business financials, property value where
applicable, existing liabilities, and lender policy.

### How long does loan approval take?

Processing time varies by product, lender, documentation, verification,
and applicant profile. Do not publish a fixed approval time unless
BankFin can substantiate it.

### Does BankFin provide the loan directly?

Clarify BankFin's exact business model before publishing this statement.
If BankFin acts as a financial intermediary/DSA/loan advisor rather than
a lender, say so clearly.

### What is an unsecured business loan?

It is a business financing facility that generally does not require
traditional property collateral, although lenders may impose other
eligibility, security, guarantee, or documentation requirements.

### What is a loan against property?

It is a secured financing facility where an eligible residential or
commercial property is offered as security to the lender, subject to
valuation, legal verification, and lender criteria.

### Can I apply if I am self-employed?

Eligibility depends on the lender and product. Self-employed applicants
may be assessed using business vintage, income, banking history,
financial statements, tax records, credit profile, and other criteria.

### Will applying affect my credit score?

The impact depends on how the lender performs credit checks and the
application process. Customers should understand whether a formal credit
enquiry will be made before proceeding.

### What interest rate will I receive?

Interest rates vary by lender, product, applicant profile,
creditworthiness, tenure, loan amount, and other factors. The applicable
lender determines the final rate.

### Are there processing fees?

Fees vary by lender and product. Customers should review the applicable
fee schedule and terms before accepting an offer.

# 28. CONTENT STRATEGY SUMMARY

The new BankFin website should answer five questions immediately:

**1. What does BankFin do?**\
BankFin helps customers explore and navigate financing solutions.

**2. Who is it for?**\
Individuals, home buyers, entrepreneurs, MSMEs and businesses.

**3. What can I get help with?**\
Personal loans, home loans, business loans, MSME financing, overdrafts,
project finance and loans against property.

**4. Why should I trust BankFin?**\
Personalized guidance, transparent communication, multiple financing
options and customer-focused assistance.

**5. What should I do next?**\
Start an enquiry or speak with an advisor.

The website should make those five answers obvious within the first few
seconds of a visitor landing on the homepage.
